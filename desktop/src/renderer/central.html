About Riyeser Central and your privacy

Riyeser Central only reads the other programs’ records on this PC to show your home screen; they stay on this PC. It checks GitHub for updates, and talks to Riyeser online only for your account, your business’s licence and, unless you turn it off above, the anonymous usage counts. Demo mode is never counted.
