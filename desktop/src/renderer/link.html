How to add a desk phone
  1. Plug the phone into a network socket on the same office network as this PC.
  2. Many desk phones don't come with a power adapter and expect power over the network cable (PoE). Buy the phone's power adapter, or a small PoE switch.
  3. Turn the phone on. Yealink, Grandstream, Snom and Polycom phones ask the network who sets them up, and appear here as New. Tick them and choose Give to…, then restart the phone if it hasn't set itself up within a minute.
  4. Wi-Fi desk phones work too, once joined to the office Wi-Fi.
  5. A phone at another site or at home reaches it through the Riyeser relay: see Settings → Connect your branches.

Phone didn't appear? Add it by its MAC address (12 letters and numbers on a sticker under the phone), or many at once with Add several phones (paste a list or import a CSV).
