Desk phones

Add a desk phone
  1. Plug the phone into a network socket on the same office network as this PC.
  2. Many desk phones don't come with a power adapter and expect power over the network cable (PoE). Buy the phone's power adapter, or a small PoE switch.
  3. Turn the phone on. Yealink, Grandstream, Snom and Polycom phones ask the network who sets them up, and appear here as New phone found. Choose who it's for, then restart the phone if it hasn't set itself up within a minute.
  4. Wi-Fi desk phones work too, once joined to the office Wi-Fi.
  5. A phone in another building or at home won't reach it; that's coming with the cloud version.

Phone didn't appear? Add it by its MAC address (12 letters and numbers on a sticker under the phone), or use Set up a phone by hand above to type the details into any SIP phone.
