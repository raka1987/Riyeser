Getting started

  1. Setup → Company: enter your business name, ABN, address and when your financial year starts.
  2. Add your customers and suppliers, and your stock items with buy and sell prices.
  3. Already trading? Enter opening balances: Setup → Accounts for bank balances, and opening quantity and cost on each new stock item.
  4. Start invoicing. The Dashboard shows cash, who owes you, bills due and low stock.

Every transaction is double-entry, so the reports always balance. You never type debits and credits for normal work.

Invoices & customers

  • New Invoice: choose a customer, then add lines. Picking a stock item fills in the price, account and GST. Tick Prices include GST if your prices are GST-inclusive.
  • Save as lets you keep a Quote or Order. Open it later and press Convert to invoice.
  • Receive Payments: pick the customer and enter what they paid against each invoice. One payment can cover several invoices.
  • Made a mistake? A posted invoice can't be edited. Void it (this reverses the books and returns the stock) and enter it again. Void any payment on it first.
  • Download PDF gives you a PDF to email. Statement on the customer's card shows everything they owe.

Stock & suppliers

  • Tick I inventory this item to track quantity and cost. Riyeser uses average cost: each purchase updates the average, and each sale records cost of goods sold automatically.
  • Receive Stock (Bill): enter the supplier's bill with the stock items and quantities. Stock goes up and you owe the supplier. Use Pay Bills when you pay.
  • Stocktake: type what you counted. The differences are posted as an inventory adjustment.
  • Set a reorder level on each item to see it on the dashboard and in Items to Reorder when stock runs low.
  • The Category (e.g. Coffee, Hardware) decides which tab the item shows under in Riyeser POS.

Riyeser POS

  • Open register opens the full-screen till. Tap products (or scan a barcode), check the Current sale, then tap Pay. Choose Cash or Card; for cash, tap the note the customer gave and the till shows the change.
  • POS setup (Shop counter → POS setup, owners and administrators only) sets the business type: Retail or Restaurant. Retail never shows tables or the kitchen. Each type has switches, such as barcode scanning, stock on tiles, tables, kitchen dockets and discounts. You can also name your tills here.
  • Retail: scan a barcode, or type a SKU and press Enter, or tap tiles. Hold puts a sale aside while the customer fetches something; find it again under Held sales.
  • Restaurant: choose Dine-in and a table, or Takeaway. Tap a line in the order to change the quantity, add options or a kitchen note (e.g. oat milk), or give a discount. Send to kitchen prints a docket. Tables with an open bill show amber in the table picker.
  • Photos and Favourites: in Inventory, edit a product to add a photo and tick Show in POS Favourites. Favourites are the first tab on the till.
  • Sales shows the day: totals, GST, sales by hour, best sellers and every sale with Receipt and Void. Only users with Full access to Sales can void.

Money & banking

  • Money Out / Money In: payments and deposits that aren't for an invoice or bill (rent, bank fees, interest). Amounts include GST unless you untick it.
  • Bank Reconciliation ({{ editions.enterprise.name }}): enter your statement's closing balance and tick the transactions that appear on it until the difference is $0.00.
  • Bank Statement Import ({{ editions.enterprise.name }}): load a CSV from your bank, then allocate each line to an account in one click.
  • Banking your cash takings: use Transfer Money from Undeposited Funds to your bank account when you deposit the till cash.

Payroll

  • With Riyeser Payroll: finished pay runs appear under Import from Payroll. Import each one to record wages, tax withheld and employer contributions.
  • Built-in payroll ({{ editions.enterprise.name }}): add employees, then Run Payroll. PAYG is an estimate using Australian 2024-25 rates, so check it before lodging.

Reports

  • Every report can be filtered by date, printed and exported to CSV. Click a line to drill down to the transactions behind it.
  • Who Owes Me (aged receivables) groups unpaid invoices by how overdue they are.
  • GST Summary shows the figures for your BAS: G1, 1A, 1B and so on.
  • Inventory Report shows what's on hand at average cost. Its total should match the Inventory account; Support Diagnostics checks this for you.

Accounts & journals

  • Chart of Accounts: click any account to see every transaction in it.
  • General Journal ({{ editions.enterprise.name }}): for adjustments such as depreciation. Debits must equal credits.
  • Nothing is ever deleted. Corrections are reversals, so the audit trail stays complete.

Setup & editions

  • {{ editions.basic.name }} (free): invoices, quotes, customers, stock, suppliers, simple banking, POS and the key reports.
  • {{ editions.enterprise.name }} (included in the free trial, then needs a licence): everything in {{ editions.basic.name }} plus bank reconciliation, statement import, payroll, journals, jobs, budgets, Balance Sheet and Trial Balance.
  • Switch any time under Setup → Company → Edition. Your data never changes. If the trial ends without a licence, companies open in {{ editions.basic.name }}; enter a licence in Riyeser Central and {{ editions.enterprise.name }} comes back with everything as you left it.
  • Lock periods up to stops anyone posting into a finished period, for example after your BAS is lodged.
  • Riyeser folder: tick Save a PDF of every invoice and bill to file copies in Documents\Riyeser for Riyeser Desk Assistant.

Users & passwords

  • Roles: Admin can do everything. Accountant can do all transactions but not manage users. Viewer is read-only.
  • Forgot password? on the sign-in screen works in the desktop app on the computer that holds the company file. Otherwise an admin can reset it under Users & Roles.
  • Back up the company file regularly. It's the single file company.db in your .riyeser folder.

Other Riyeser modules

  • Riyeser Payroll sends pay runs to Accounting through Documents\Riyeser\Inbox\Payroll.
  • Riyeser Desk Assistant files the PDFs Accounting saves in Documents\Riyeser\Accounting.
  • All modules open from the Riyeser hub.

Troubleshooting

  • "Period is locked": the date is on or before the lock date. Change the date, or an admin can move the lock date.
  • "Out of balance" on a journal: debits must equal credits. The difference is shown under the lines.
  • Reconciliation won't balance: look for a missing or doubled transaction, or one dated after the statement date.
  • "That's part of Riyeser Accounting {{ editions.enterprise.name }}": the screen isn't in {{ editions.basic.name }}. An admin can switch under Setup → Company. After the free trial, {{ editions.enterprise.name }} needs a licence from Riyeser Central.
  • Still stuck? Open Support Diagnostics, press Download, and send the file to support. It contains no customer or bank details.
