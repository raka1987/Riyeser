Getting started

  1. Setup → POS setup: choose Retail or Restaurant, turn on the features you want and name your tills.
  2. Products → New product: add what you sell, with a price, a category (it becomes a tab on the till) and, if you like, a photo. Tick I inventory this item to count stock.
  3. Setup → Users & roles: add a sign-in for each staff member. Managers (Full access to Sales) can approve voids, refunds and big discounts.
  4. Tap Open the till and start selling.

Riyeser POS and Riyeser Accounting

  • POS and Accounting are separate apps that open the same company file (in your .riyeser folder). Install either, or both.
  • Each till sale is saved as a paid tax invoice to the Walk-in Customer (or the customer you picked), so Accounting's reports, stock and GST include it straight away.
  • Products, stock and customers are shared: change a price in either app and the other sees it.
  • Managers see a low on stock note on the till when products reach their reorder level, and after a sale that takes one there. It never stops a sale. Tap it for the list; purchase orders are made in Riyeser Accounting.
  • Bank accounts, bills, payroll and the reports live in Riyeser Accounting.

Users & passwords

  • Forgot your password? Click Forgot password? on the sign-in screen, on the computer that holds the company file. An administrator can also set a new one under Setup → Users & roles.
