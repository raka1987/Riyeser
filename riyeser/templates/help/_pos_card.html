Riyeser POS

  • Riyeser POS is its own app, with its own installer and its own icon (the teal Po.). It opens the same company file as Riyeser Accounting, so every till sale shows in Accounting straight away as a paid invoice, and stock, the cash drawer{{ ' and ' ~ tl.word if tl.has_tax }} stay right. You don't need Accounting to use POS.
  • Free for 90 days. Then Riyeser POS is {{ prices.pos }} a month per business. In the last two weeks the till reminds you how many days are left; after the trial, new sales stop until you enter a Riyeser licence in Riyeser Central (Licence). Nothing is deleted: past sales, receipts and reports stay.
  • Sell opens the full-screen till. Tap products (or scan a barcode), check the Current sale, then tap Pay. Choose Cash or Card; for cash, tap the note the customer gave and the till shows the change.
  • POS setup (Setup → POS setup in Riyeser POS, owners and administrators only) sets the business type: Retail or Restaurant. Retail never shows tables or the kitchen. Each type has switches, such as barcode scanning, stock on tiles, tables, kitchen dockets and discounts. You can also name your tills here.
  • Retail: scan a barcode, or type a SKU and press Enter, or tap tiles. Hold puts a sale aside while the customer fetches something; find it again under Held sales.
  • Restaurant: choose Dine-in and a table, or Takeaway. Tap a line in the order to change the quantity, add options or a kitchen note (e.g. oat milk), or give a discount. Send to kitchen sends what's new on the order: a printed docket, the kitchen screen, or both (see below). Items already sent are never sent again. Tables with an open bill show amber in the table picker.
  • Photos and Favourites: in Inventory, edit a product to add a photo and tick Show in POS Favourites. Favourites are the first tab on the till.
  • Sales shows the day: totals, {{ tl.word ~ ', ' if tl.has_tax }}sales by hour, best sellers and every sale with Receipt, Refund and Void.
  • Manager approvals (Setup → Manager approvals in Riyeser POS): managers are the people with Full access to Sales. Give each a PIN and, if you like, a manager card (any barcode card, scanned with the till's scanner). Staff then need a manager's PIN or card to void a sale, give a refund, give a discount over the limit, or close a till that doesn't balance. The manager approves on the same screen. Staff stay logged in. Every approval, with its reason, is kept in the override log. Lost a card? Disable it there straight away.
  • Refunds: on the Sales screen, tap Refund, choose the items coming back and how the money goes back. Stock returns to the shelf, and the income{{ ' and ' ~ tl.word if tl.has_tax }} come{{ 's' if not tl.has_tax }} off.
  • Open and close the till: the first time you sell, the till asks for the float (the change you start with). At the end of the day choose Close till, count the notes and coins, and Riyeser shows if the drawer is over or short. Closing prints the Z-report, records any difference in the Cash Over/Short account, and records the takings going to the bank. Print mid-day report (X-read) shows the figures without closing.
  • Card payments (EFTPOS): choose how each till takes cards in POS setup → Card payments. Not linked works as before. Manual is for your own machine that isn't connected: the till shows the amount, you key it into the machine, then tap Approved or Declined (and, if you like, the last 4 digits and auth code from the slip). Practice terminal is a pretend machine on the screen for training and demos: approve, decline, cancel, time out, add a tip or cash out. No money moves. Demo companies use it automatically. Linked terminals from card providers (Stripe Terminal, Windcave and others) are coming soon. Use Test a $1.00 payment to check a till.
  • Taking a card: tap Pay, then Card, then Send to card machine. The till shows Waiting for customer… tap, insert or swipe with the amount and a Cancel button. Approved finishes the sale, and the receipt shows the card type, the last 4 digits, the auth code and the machine's reference. Declined keeps the sale open: try again or choose another way to pay. The cash drawer is only needed for cash, change or cash out.
  • Split payments: tap Pay part by card (split), type the card amount and send it. When it's approved, the till shows what's left to pay: take it in cash (or another card).
  • Card refunds go back through the card machine (or Manual) before they're recorded. A sale paid on a card machine can't be voided; refund it instead, so the money goes back to the card.
  • If Riyeser closes during a card payment, nothing is lost: when the till opens again it lists the unfinished payment. Check the card machine, then choose It shows approved or It shows not charged, and Finish the sale (or Refund it to the card). A card payment can only ever be used on one sale.
  • Close till shows card terminal totals by card type (Visa, Mastercard, EFTPOS, ...) with tips, cash out and surcharges, so you can compare them with the card machine's settlement. Cash out given on card payments comes off the cash expected in the drawer.
  • Product variants (size, colour, ...): edit a product and choose Add variants. Give it up to 3 options (for example Colour: Navy, White and Size: S, M, L), then Generate combinations. Each combination is its own item with its own SKU, barcode, price, cost and stock, so stock value, cost of sales and Sales by item work per variant. On the till, tapping the product opens a quick picker; scanning a variant's barcode adds it straight away. Export CSV and Import CSV (Products › Variants) let you change many at once. A product without variants works as before.
  • Stock locations and transfers: everything starts at Main. Add more under Products › Stock locations and choose which location each till sells from (POS setup › Tills). A transfer goes Draft → Sent (in transit) → Received, and can arrive in parts; print the transfer docket to go with the stock. Transfers don't change your books: the Inventory account stays the same. Stocktake and Low stock can be done per location, with each location's own reorder levels.
  • Tables: move, merge and split: open a table's order and tap Table. Move table moves the whole order; Move items moves some items (or some of a quantity); Merge tables joins two orders. Split the bill evenly between guests, by item (tap each guest's items, or Share one), or by amounts. Each guest pays their part by cash, card or both, and the table closes when every part is paid. Every move and split is kept in the order's history and the audit trail.
  • Setting up tables (Setup › Tables, for restaurants): add your areas (Inside, Outside) and tables. Type 1-10 to add tables 1 to 10 at once, or names separated by commas. Change a table's name, seats or area at any time; a table with an open order can't be removed until the order is finished or moved.
  • Promotions (Setup › Promotions): take a percentage or an amount off a product, a category or the whole sale, with optional start and end dates, days and times (a happy hour), and an optional promo code. The till applies a running promotion by itself and the receipt shows it. Each item gets its one best promotion; a promotion with a code counts once the cashier types the code under Promo code.
  • Looking at a past receipt: on Sales (or All sales) tap View receipt to see it on the screen, print it again or email it. After a sale, View or email the receipt does the same. Receipts always open inside Riyeser, never in the web browser.
  • Printers, cash drawer and scanner are all under Setup › Hardware: choose the receipt printer, the paper width (58 or 80 mm), margins and logo size, and print a test. If no receipt printer is chosen, receipts use the Windows print box; on an office (A4) printer they print as a narrow strip.
  • Cash drawer (Setup › Hardware › Cash drawer): works with drawers that plug into an ESC/POS receipt printer installed in Windows on this PC. The drawer opens on cash sales (once per sale), cash refunds, and No sale (needs a reason; staff need a manager). Use Test: open the drawer first. A drawer with its own USB cable isn't supported.
  • Kitchen screen (Setup › Kitchen & screens): choose for each station (Kitchen, Bar, ...) whether orders print, show on the kitchen screen, or both. Turn on Kitchen screens on this network, open the address shown on a tablet or PC in the kitchen, and type the pairing code. The screen shows tickets by station with notes, table or takeaway, and how long they've waited (amber, then red). Tap an item or Bump order when it's done; Recall brings back the last one. Moved and voided items are marked. It shows no prices or sales. Staff who only use the kitchen screen can have the Kitchen staff role.
