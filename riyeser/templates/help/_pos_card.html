Riyeser POS

  • Riyeser POS is its own app, with its own installer and its own icon (the teal Po.). It opens the same company file as Riyeser Accounting, so every till sale shows in Accounting straight away as a paid invoice, and stock, the cash drawer and GST stay right. You don't need Accounting to use POS.
  • Free for your first 500 sales. The till warns you once 400 have been used. After 500, new sales stop until you enter a Riyeser licence in Riyeser Central (Licence). Nothing is deleted: past sales, receipts, close-till reports, products and stock all stay. Voided sales don't count.
  • Sell opens the full-screen till. Tap products (or scan a barcode), check the Current sale, then tap Pay. Choose Cash or Card; for cash, tap the note the customer gave and the till shows the change.
  • POS setup (Setup → POS setup in Riyeser POS, owners and administrators only) sets the business type: Retail or Restaurant. Retail never shows tables or the kitchen. Each type has switches, such as barcode scanning, stock on tiles, tables, kitchen dockets and discounts. You can also name your tills here.
  • Retail: scan a barcode, or type a SKU and press Enter, or tap tiles. Hold puts a sale aside while the customer fetches something; find it again under Held sales.
  • Restaurant: choose Dine-in and a table, or Takeaway. Tap a line in the order to change the quantity, add options or a kitchen note (e.g. oat milk), or give a discount. Send to kitchen prints a docket. Tables with an open bill show amber in the table picker.
  • Photos and Favourites: in Inventory, edit a product to add a photo and tick Show in POS Favourites. Favourites are the first tab on the till.
  • Sales shows the day: totals, GST, sales by hour, best sellers and every sale with Receipt, Refund and Void.
  • Manager approvals (Setup → Manager approvals in Riyeser POS): managers are the people with Full access to Sales. Give each a PIN and, if you like, a manager card (any barcode card, scanned with the till's scanner). Staff then need a manager's PIN or card to void a sale, give a refund, give a discount over the limit, or close a till that doesn't balance. The manager approves on the same screen. Staff stay logged in. Every approval, with its reason, is kept in the override log. Lost a card? Disable it there straight away.
  • Refunds: on the Sales screen, tap Refund, choose the items coming back and how the money goes back. Stock returns to the shelf, and the income and GST come off.
  • Open and close the till: the first time you sell, the till asks for the float (the change you start with). At the end of the day choose Close till, count the notes and coins, and Riyeser shows if the drawer is over or short. Closing prints the Z-report, records any difference in the Cash Over/Short account, and records the takings going to the bank. Print mid-day report (X-read) shows the figures without closing.
