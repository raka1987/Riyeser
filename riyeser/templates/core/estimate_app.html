Riyeser Estimate

Measure jobs on a plan, a photo or the map, and turn them into quotes.

Estimate is its own app. It opens the same company file as this one, so your customers are already there, and the quotes it makes show here straight away, ready to turn into an invoice{% if product.key != 'invoices' %} or a job{% endif %}.

  • Type in sizes: rooms (walls less doors and windows), areas, lengths, counts and hours. Works with a laser measurer that types into the box.
  • On a plan or photo: set the scale, then draw lengths, areas and counts.
  • On the map: draw roofs (with pitch), fences, driveways and lawns on satellite imagery.
  • Trade templates for roofing, fencing, painting, tiling, concreting and more turn the sizes into priced quote lines.
