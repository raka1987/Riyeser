Cash drawer

Open the cash drawer by itself on cash sales, cash refunds and “No sale”.

POS setupOpen the till
ⓘ
Works with drawers that plug into an ESC/POS receipt printer (the cable from the drawer goes into the printer's drawer port, often marked DK). Riyeser sends the printer the standard drawer pulse (ESC p 0 25 250). The printer must be installed in Windows on this computer. A drawer with its own USB cable, or a printer that doesn't understand ESC/POS, isn't supported: open it with its key. Not yet tested with a list of named models, so use the test button first.

When the drawer opens

  • A sale paid (or part paid) in cash, or with cash out on the card machine: once per sale, even if the Pay button is tapped twice.
  • A refund paid back in cash.
  • No sale on the till: needs a reason. Staff need a manager's PIN or card; every one is in the list below and in Manager approvals.
