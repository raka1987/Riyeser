Installing & updating

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new programs until they are digitally signed, which we are arranging. It is safe to continue if you downloaded the file from riyeser.com.

Do I need an administrator password?

No. Each program installs just for your Windows user, so no admin rights are needed.

How do I update?

Open Riyeser Central — it checks for updates and shows a banner when one is ready. Click Install updates. Or download the newest version from Downloads and run it: it installs over the old version and keeps all your information.

How do I uninstall a program?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find the program and choose Uninstall. Your documents, books and records are not deleted.

Moving to a new computer
  1. Install the programs on the new computer.
  2. Copy across your information: Accounting's .riyeser folder (in your user folder) and Payroll's Documents\Riyeser\Payroll folder. Email us if you'd like a hand.

A program I installed isn't showing

Under More Riyeser apps, click Locate… next to its name and pick the program (or its desktop shortcut). Central remembers it from then on.

The Money panel is empty

It reads from Riyeser Accounting. Open Accounting, set up your company and enter an invoice or bill — the totals then appear in Central.

My meetings don't show under Today

Open Desk Assistant → Settings → Calendars and connect your calendar.

Accounting

I forgot my password
  1. On the sign-in screen, click Forgot password?
  2. Choose your username and a new password.

This works in the Accounting program on the computer that holds your company file, so nobody elsewhere can use it. Your books are not changed — only the password. For other staff, an administrator can set a new password under Setup → Users & Roles.

Where are my books kept?

In one company file on your computer: .riyeser\company.db in your Windows user folder. Copy that file to a USB stick or cloud drive to keep a backup.

Starting with an empty company

When you first open Accounting it asks for your company name. Leave Load demo data unticked to start completely empty.

Run several companies (groups, trusts or clients)
  1. Click Companies in the menu, then Add company. Each company gets its own books, users and access.
  2. Switch company from its name at the top of the screen. Companies also shows cash, money owed, bills and profit for every company and in total.
  3. To give a staff member the same sign-in in other companies, tick them under Same sign-in and access in on Setup → Users & roles. Their access can then differ company by company.

Already have a company file from another computer or a client? Use Bring in an existing company file. It is copied in; the original isn't changed. Archived companies are kept and can be restored any time.

Limit what each staff member can see or change
  1. Sign in as an administrator and open Setup → Users & roles.
  2. For each person, choose a level for every area — Sales, Purchases, Banking, Stock, Payroll, Accounts & reports and Setup: Hidden (not shown at all), View (look and print), Add & edit (but not delete or void) or Full.
  3. Or click a quick set — Owner, Bookkeeper, Sales staff, Payroll officer or Read-only — then adjust and Save.

Hidden areas really are hidden: their menus, figures, reports and wages entries don't appear, and their pages are refused. Every change is recorded in the audit trail.

Printing or exporting reports

Every report has Print and Export CSV buttons, so you can send figures to your accountant or open them in Excel.

More help inside Accounting

Click Help at the top of any Accounting screen for a guide to invoices, banking, GST/BAS and reports.

Payroll

Where are payslips saved?

As PDFs in Documents\Riyeser\Payroll\Payslips. Open that folder from the Payroll menu.

Are my payroll records backed up?

Yes — Payroll saves a backup every day in Documents\Riyeser\Payroll\Backups and keeps the last 30.

A payslip PDF looks blank

Make sure you have the newest version of Payroll, then export the payslip again. If it still looks blank, open it in Riyeser PDF or email us the payslip date (not the file).

Services

On this PC or with an online account?

The Windows app asks the first time you open it. On this PC: no account, projects saved on your computer, works with no internet. With my online account: sign in and projects sync with phones, the website and your team. Change it any time from the Riyeser Services menu → How I work…

Moving my PC projects online

Menu Riyeser Services → Sync to my online account…, then sign in. Your projects and photos upload and the copy on the PC is kept as a backup.

I forgot my online password

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password and follow the email. Projects saved on your PC are not affected.

Working without signal on site

Keep working — changes are saved on the device and sync when you are back online. The status at the top shows All saved or how many changes are waiting.

Desk Assistant

Desk Assistant moved a file — where is it?

Open Where are my files?. Every move is listed with an Open button, and Put everything back returns every file to where it was.

Finding a file

Open Find a file and type part of the name, a word inside it, a company or an old name. Narrow it down by type and year.

Undoing a tidy-up

On the Today page under Recent tidy-ups, click Undo. Nothing is ever deleted — duplicates are moved to an Archive folder, not removed.
