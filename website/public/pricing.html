Questions

How does the free trial work?

Install Riyeser Central and every program is free for 90 days. No account and no card. Riyeser Central shows how many days are left.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged. Everything you made stays on your computer and can still be opened. To keep adding new work, get a licence and enter the key in Riyeser Central.

What counts as a user?

Each person who uses Riyeser on their own computer. One licence covers every Riyeser program for that person.

Can I change the number of users later?

Yes, add or remove users any time. Your price per user follows the table above.

How do extra companies work?

Each licence includes one company in Riyeser Accounting. Groups, trusts and bookkeepers can add more for $10 a month per extra company, and switch between them with one sign-in. If you stop paying for an extra company, its books stay — they open read-only until you add it back.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep everything you made, and can always open and export it.

How does the early bird offer work?

The first 100 users to buy a licence pay half price for their first 12 months — for example $10 instead of $20 per user a month. After 12 months the normal price applies. Once 100 users have the offer, it ends; we won't extend it or run a fake countdown.

How do I buy?

Online checkout is opening soon. Until then, email info@riyeser.com with your business name and number of users and we'll send your licence key.
