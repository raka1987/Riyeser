Questions

What does it cost?

Every program is free for 90 days with no card. After that it's $20 per user a month for the whole suite, less for teams of 5 or more, and 2 months free if you pay yearly. Nothing is deleted when the trial ends, and Accounting Basics stays free. See Pricing.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Riyeser Services can optionally sync to an online account so phones, clients and staff can see your projects.

Will it work on a Mac?

Not yet — the programs are for Windows 10 and 11. Riyeser Services also works in any web browser at riyeser.com/app.

What if I get stuck?

The Help Centre has step-by-step answers for every program, or email info@riyeser.com and a real person will reply.
