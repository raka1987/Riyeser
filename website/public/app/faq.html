About Riyeser

What is Riyeser?

An app for marking up building services on your drawings while they're installed. You record every cable, pipe and duct, connect each one from end to end, run inspections, add photos and test results, and print the handover pack, as-builts, schematics and port schedules at the end.

Who is it for?

Electrical, data and comms, fire, plumbing, drainage, mechanical, security and AV contractors, plus the project managers, supervisors and clients who need to see what's been installed and checked.

Which trades does it cover?

Data and comms, electrical, fire, water, drainage, mechanical, security, audio visual, gas, and risers and trenches. Each trade has its own items, symbols, checklists and reports.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Open the demo to explore a marked-up resort with a guided tour. Nothing is saved, and it resets each time you visit.

Using it on site

Which devices does it work on?

Phones, tablets and computers, in any modern browser. On a phone or tablet you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything you record is saved on your device straight away, and it syncs with your team when you're back in range. The status at the top shows when everything is saved.

What drawings can I use?

PDF files and images such as PNG or JPG. Each page becomes a level you can mark up. Set the scale once and lengths are measured for you.

Do the symbols follow Australian Standards?

The plan symbols are based on AS/NZS 1102.11 conventions for electrical and communications items and AS 1670 for fire. You can also switch on AS 5488 service colours in Settings. Tap Key on the plan to see what each symbol means.

Can I connect an outlet to a specific patch panel port?

Yes. Open the outlet, choose how many ports it has, then pick the rack, panel and port number for each one. The cable, terminations and test results are recorded end to end, and they show up in the schematic and port schedule.

Sharing and handover

What can I print or export?

A full handover pack, as-built drawings, schematics for any service, item or area, port and circuit schedules, labels, RFIs, defect reports and test reports. Each prints straight to PDF.

Can a client answer an RFI without an account?

Yes. Send them a client link for that RFI or defect. They can see it and reply, but nothing else in your project.

Who can see my projects?

Only people your admin adds to your team, at the access level they're given.

Your information

Who owns my project information?

You do, or your client does, as you've agreed between you. We only store and process it to run Riyeser for you. See the terms of use.

Can I share or copy Riyeser itself?

No. Riyeser, its software, designs, templates and content belong to Riyeser Group and may not be copied or shared without written permission. Your own reports are yours to use on your projects.
