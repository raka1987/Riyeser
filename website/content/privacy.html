Privacy policy

Last updated [date of publishing].

This policy explains what personal information Riyeser collects, why, where it goes, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We've written it in plain English and kept it to what our software really does.

The short version

  • Your books stay on your PC. Accounting, invoices, payroll, POS sales, bookings, documents and backups are kept on your own computer. We can't see them. Only what you choose to send reaches us (for example, online connections or a problem report).
  • Online, we keep what's needed to run your account: your sign-in, your business details, your team's chat and files in Riyeser Link, your plan and billing, and your help requests.
  • Calls aren't recorded. Call sound and video go directly between devices, or through Riyeser's own relay when they can't connect directly. They're never recorded or kept.
  • No cookies for tracking, no ads, and we don't sell personal information. Our website counts visits without cookies and without keeping your internet address. Browsers that ask not to be tracked aren't counted.
  • When your business uses Riyeser's online connections (for example, the booking widget on its website), the business decides what happens to its customers' details. We only carry them for the business.

What stays on your PC

Riyeser's Windows programs work offline and keep your records in files on your own computer. That includes your ledgers, invoices, quotes and estimates, customers and suppliers, payroll and employee details, POS sales, bookings, stock, documents sorted by Riyeser Documents, Riyeser Link Office's list of recent calls (kept 90 days), and the automatic backups each program makes (in your Documents folder; the last 30 are kept). None of this is sent to us. You look after it, including your own backups and who can use your PC.

Some things leave your PC only when you choose to use them:

  • Online connections: if your business switches them on, Riyeser Central on one of your PCs collects what your website sends and publishes what you choose for your website (see Online connections).
  • Problem reports: when you press Report an issue or Get help, you see exactly what will be sent before you send it (see Support).
  • Features that talk to other services directly (see Optional features).

Calls

Calls in Riyeser Link (in the browser or the app) connect directly between the two devices where possible. When they can't, the sound and video pass through Riyeser's own relay servers. Either way the call is encrypted by the browser's standard calling technology (WebRTC), and calls are never recorded or kept. To start a call, small set-up messages pass through Riyeser online: who is calling whom, in which business, when, and technical connection details, which include the devices' network addresses. The app removes them when the call ends. [CHECK: set-up messages left behind (for example if a browser closes mid-call) aren't removed automatically yet. Consider a daily clean-up, then state a period here.] Your list of recent calls is kept in your own browser, not with us.

Desk phones with Riyeser Link Office are run by the Link Office program on your office PC. Every 5 minutes it tells Riyeser online how it's going, so owners and admins can manage phones from the website: the PC's name, version and network address(es), the PC's network card (MAC) address, the desk phones (make, model, MAC address, extension, the name, location and note you gave them, and their address on your office network), the extensions, how many calls are in progress and whether each extension is on a call. It never sends numbers dialled, call lists, recordings or sound. Desk phone passwords stay on your office PC. When an owner or admin presses Show, the password is encrypted for that browser tab only, and our copy is wiped as soon as it's read or after 5 minutes.

The Riyeser relay joins your sites and phones at home. Call sound between sites passes through it but is never looked at, logged or kept. Phones at home sign in with their own username, and we keep only a one-way digest of the password. The relay's log records which sites and phones connected, from what internet address, extensions and how calls ended, but not call sound, passwords or set-up details. Support

When you ask for help we keep your request: your name and email (if you're not signed in), the subject, the messages between you and us, and any rating or comment. Our team may add internal notes, including notes of phone calls with you.

If you send a problem report from Riyeser Central or Riyeser Link, it includes which program and version, the other Riyeser programs on that PC, the Windows version, the error you typed, and the last lines (200 at most) of that program's technical log. Online connections (for your business's own customers)

A business can connect its own website to its Riyeser programs with the Riyeser API, webhooks or the booking widget. When a visitor to that website books, orders, makes an enquiry, signs up, accepts a quote or pays an invoice, what they type (for example their name, email, phone, address, notes, and whether they agreed to marketing) is sent to Riyeser online and held in the business's post box until the business's PC collects it and files it in the business's own records. The business also publishes its services, prices, stock levels and free booking times for its website to show.

We also keep a short log of API requests (the route, the result and the time, and only a shortened internet address such as 203.0.113.0, never the request itself or the key), and the webhook messages sent to the business's own systems. API keys are kept only as a one-way hash.

For this information, the business is in charge (it is the "controller"). We carry it for the business, on its instructions (we are its "processor" or service provider). If you made a booking or order through a business's website, please contact that business about your information. The booking widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the visitor's browser.

Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and each page asks GitHub for the newest release so the download buttons stay current. How we protect it

  • Everything between your browser or PC and Riyeser online is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database rules mean each person only sees their own business, and only the chats they're in.
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. Owners and admins need it to use their admin powers, and our staff need it to use Riyeser's staff tools.
  • Passwords, API keys and phone passwords are kept only as one-way hashes or digests. Licences are digitally signed so they can't be faked.
  • Nightly backups are encrypted (AES-256) and kept with a different company from our database provider.
  • Our staff tools have permissions per role, a history that can't be changed, and two people for refunds over a set amount. Support access to your phones lasts one hour and is recorded.
  • Problem reports are scrubbed of emails, long numbers and passwords, on your PC and again when saved. API logs keep only a shortened internet address.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell the people affected and the regulator, as the law requires.
